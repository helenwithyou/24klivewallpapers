Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Service provider: icreativeart Co., Ltd.
info@icreativeart.com

Explore FoldMuse without creating an account. Saving a wallpaper uses add-only Photos access: we do not read or upload your existing photos. Reminders are optional and stay under your control. This policy explains the information used to provide these services.

1. Information we process

FoldMuse does not require registration. When you use the app, we and our service providers automatically receive installation identifiers, download records, purchase and subscription information, and the wallpaper or page from which a purchase starts. These records support free allowances, subscription access and internal usage statistics.

We also process notification identifiers, permission and reminder settings, language, time zone and delivery results. Language and time zone may be synchronized when reminders are off. Our services receive technical information such as IP address, device/software information and error logs. If you contact us, we receive your email address and message.

Apple handles payments. We do not receive your full payment-card details or Apple Account password.

2. How we use it

We use this information to provide downloads, verify and restore subscriptions, prevent duplicate operations and misuse, send reminders you enable, provide support and understand service usage. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

Where applicable, processing is based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in security and proportionate service statistics, your consent for optional reminders, or legal obligations. Reading this policy does not give consent to unrelated processing.

3. Photos and optional reminders

Photos access is add-only: FoldMuse saves requested wallpapers without reading or uploading your existing photos. Preview effects are processed on your device. You can change Photos permission in iOS Settings.

New-wallpaper, activity and promotional reminders require your in-app opt-in and iOS authorization. Turn them off in FoldMuse or iOS Settings at any time without losing subscription benefits. We do not request GPS location.

Image caches and preferences are stored on your device. These policy pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts added by us; their hosting provider receives ordinary connection information.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use payment and subscription services to process purchases and verify access; cloud hosting, storage and content-delivery services to operate the app and store the records described above; and notification and email services to deliver enabled reminders and respond to you. We share only information needed for these purposes. Providers acting for us must protect it at least as described in this policy. Payment platforms may also process information under their own privacy policies.

We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service. Information may be processed outside your country. Where required by law, we use appropriate transfer safeguards, such as approved contractual protections; you can contact us for details.

5. Retention and security

We retain installation and download records while needed to manage access and free allowances; purchase records while needed for subscriptions, refunds, disputes and legal obligations; and notification and support records while needed to honor your settings and resolve requests. We delete or de-identify information when no longer needed, subject to legally required retention and backup retention periods.

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect information.

6. Your rights

Contact info@icreativeart.com to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may also request portability, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. We may need information to verify your request and locate records associated with your installation.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes, with further notice or consent where required. Privacy questions: info@icreativeart.com.
