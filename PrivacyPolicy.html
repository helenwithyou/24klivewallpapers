Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 12, 2026

Welcome to 24KLiveWallpapers! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

To provide wallpaper browsing, downloading, and subscription services, we may collect:

  • Device Information: device model, OS version, device identifier, IP address, etc., used to ensure normal service operation and verify subscription benefits
  • Photo Library Access: used to save downloaded wallpapers to your device album. We do not upload your photos
  • Subscription Information: Apple In-App Purchase status, used to confirm your VIP benefits. Payment information is processed by Apple; we do not collect your payment card details
  • Usage Data: app open, browsing, and download records, as well as crash logs, used to improve the app experience
  • Advertising-related Data: to display ads to you, advertising partners may collect device information for ad performance analysis
  • Push Notifications: used to send you wallpaper updates, event reminders, and other notifications

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information only to:

  • Provide and maintain wallpaper services
  • Verify your subscription status
  • Improve app stability and user experience
  • Display ads and push relevant content

3. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services. These service providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Apple App Store: processes subscription purchases
  • Google AdMob: displays advertisements
  • Facebook SDK: ad performance statistics
  • Firebase: app analytics and crash reporting
  • JPush: push notifications
  • Amazon S3: wallpaper resource storage and distribution

4. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Manage permissions such as Photos, Notifications, and Location in your device system settings
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID account settings
  • Contact us via the email at the end of this policy for privacy-related questions

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app or on our official website. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
